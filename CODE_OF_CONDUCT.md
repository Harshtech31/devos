# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the DevOS project a welcoming, safe, and productive experience for everyone.

The community we want is described in the [Project Manifesto](PROJECT_MANIFESTO.md), and we hold ourselves to its values:

- **Curiosity** — we explore ideas before judging them.
- **Kindness** — we treat every person with respect.
- **Transparency** — we work and decide in the open.
- **Engineering Excellence** — we care about correctness, simplicity, and craft.
- **Long-Term Thinking** — we build for developers we will never meet.
- **Open Collaboration** — good ideas can come from anyone.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and offering it in return.
- Disagreeing with ideas, not with people.
- Backing technical positions with evidence, reasoning, and examples.
- Crediting others' work and acknowledging contributions.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the developers it serves.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning language of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting remarks, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Personal or political attacks directed at other participants.
- Publishing others' private information — such as addresses, contact details, or employer details — without explicit consent.
- Bad-faith participation: arguing points you do not hold, misrepresenting others' positions, or abusing process to stall or derail decisions.
- Sustained disruption of community spaces or events.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- The repository: issues, pull requests, code review, and commit discussions.
- RFC and ADR discussions, wherever they are hosted.
- Community channels announced by the project.
- Any space where an individual is representing the project or its community.

It also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public or private spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is unacceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer team through the private reporting channel announced in the repository.

Reports should include, where possible:

- What happened, with links or references to the relevant discussions.
- When and where it occurred.
- Any context that helps us understand the situation.

All reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer team will not disclose the identity of reporters without their consent, except where required to protect safety or comply with law.

Every report is acknowledged promptly and investigated fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers follow these proportionate consequences for violations. Severity depends on the nature of the violation, whether it was repeated, and whether it was intentional.

### 1. Warning

For minor violations or first offenses.

A private warning from the maintainer team explaining the violation, why the behavior was inappropriate, and what must change. Repeated violations escalate.

### 2. Temporary Ban

For serious violations, repeated minor violations, or failure to change after a warning.

The participant is excluded from interactions with the project for a defined period. No public or private interaction with the community is permitted during the ban, including on project channels and spaces. Violating a temporary ban results in a permanent ban.

### 3. Permanent Ban

For severe violations, harassment, sustained bad-faith participation, or repeated violations after temporary bans.

The participant is permanently removed from all project spaces and their contributions may be reverted where necessary.
